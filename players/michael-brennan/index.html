2026 Wyndham Championship

At Sedgefield Country Club in Greensboro, N.C., Michael Brennan closed with a 6-under 64 to hold off Beau Hossler and win the 2026 Wyndham Championship at 22 under par (258), matching the tournament's 72-hole scoring record. Ben James shot a tournament-low 9-under 61 in the final round to climb into third at -18, one shot behind Hossler, who finished second at -19. Brennan, a 24-year-old Wake Forest product, entered the week outside the top 100 in the FedExCup standings and secured a Playoffs berth with the victory — his second PGA Tour title, coming less than a year after his sponsor-exemption breakthrough at the 2025 Bank of Utah Championship. There was no DP World Tour event that week.
